A **swarm** is a population of tadpoles that swims as one large animal. Every tadpole is a real lifeform, with its own elemental heart, spindle and body prism, and the swarm's body plan is chosen by its **majority element**.

| Majority element | Creature | Tadpoles at full size |
|---|---|---|
| Mass | Whale | 192 |
| Charge | Pufferfish | 179 |
| Space | Jellyfish | 88 |
| Time | Dragonfly | 76 |

## Change the majority, change the animal

Knock out enough of a swarm's majority element and another element takes the lead. After a short pause, the swarm **morphs** into that element's creature while it's still moving, and every surviving tadpole swims to its new place in the new body.

Nothing about this is scripted. The swarm is just following the same elemental rules as everything else in the HyperSea, which is why it reacts to what you do.
