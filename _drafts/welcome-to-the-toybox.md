Cosmic Shore is a party game, but that's only half of it. The other half is **freestyle**: flying around the HyperSea with nothing to win. What fills it is **toys**.

A toy has no score and no end condition. It's something you can play with for as long as you like. The **toybox** is the set of toys you've unlocked, and they live right in the menu's freestyle world.

## Three kinds of toy

Every toy changes one thing:

- **You.** The Vessel Changer swaps the hull you fly, the Domain Changer swaps your team colours, and the Element Charger tops up the elements your hull carries. The world stays exactly where you left it.
- **Where you are.** The Cell Selector picks the world you fly in, and the Wanderway carries you from one living world to the next, with or without the Ark, a mothership you escort.
- **What you leave behind.** Connect the Dots lays a pattern of prisms that stays when you leave, and the Spawn Matrix lets creatures and plants loose into the world.

Toys don't add timers or win conditions. They work with the same rules as the rest of the game, so what happens next is up to the world and to you.
