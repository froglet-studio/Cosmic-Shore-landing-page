The game also keeps some data on your device: your settings, your consent and age answers, a copy of your saved progress, and any analytics waiting to be sent.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and the game has no voice or text chat, no ads and no in-app purchases.

Analytics and your consent

The first time you play, the game asks for your birth year. If you’re 13 or older, it then asks whether you’re happy to share gameplay analytics. We keep only your answers (13+ or not, yes or no), not your birth year.

  • If you agree, the game sends analytics to Unity Analytics and to PostHog (on PostHog’s EU servers), and turns on crash reports through Unity Cloud Diagnostics.
  • If you say no, or you’re under 13, none of this is sent. Nothing is saved up to send later.
  • To change your mind, open Settings → General → Analytics. Turning it off stops collection straight away and throws away anything not yet sent.
Turning analytics off isn’t deleting

Turning analytics off stops new data. To remove what was already sent, ask us to delete your data.

Test access requests and messages

When you ask to test an unreleased build (Windows, iPhone and iPad, or Android) on this website, we receive your email address, the platform you picked and how you heard about us. We use them only to decide on your request, send you the build or the invitation, and count which channels bring testers in. Your request is stored in our Google Workspace (Google Sheets and Gmail). To withdraw it or have it deleted, email {{ privacy_email }}.

When you send a message with the contact form on the Support or Delete My Data page, we receive your email address, the topic you picked and your message. We use them only to answer you and to act on what you asked. They are stored the same way, and kept as long as other support messages.

Children

Cosmic Shore is not directed at children under 13. Players who tell us they’re under 13 never send gameplay analytics or crash reports.

Players under 13 can still play, so the game still creates their account and saves their progress, and the display name they choose is visible to other players. If you’re a parent and want your child’s account deleted, use the Delete My Data page or email {{ privacy_email }}.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off analytics: Settings → General → Analytics.
  • Delete your account and data: see Delete My Data. Uninstalling the game doesn’t delete your account.
  • Access, correct, export or object: depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or California law), you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to fix it, or object to how we use it. Email {{ privacy_email }}. We won’t treat you differently for asking.
  • Complain: if you’re in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

This website

www.froglet.games uses Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and the Google Ads tag to count visits and measure our ad campaigns. When they run, they set cookies and send Google your IP address, browser details and the pages you visit. The trailer on the home page is a YouTube video; YouTube loads only when you press Watch the trailer.

You choose whether these cookies run. On your first visit we ask you to accept or reject them. In the EU, UK and Switzerland they stay off until you accept; elsewhere they run unless you reject them. Change your choice any time with Cookie Settings at the bottom of every page. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser.
