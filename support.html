Report a Player

Cheating, harassment, or an offensive name? Tell us who and what happened. Please include:

  • Their display name, exactly as shown in the game
  • The mode, and roughly when it happened (with your time zone)
  • What they did
  • A screenshot or clip, if you have one

Quick Answers

Before You Write In

How do I turn off analytics?

Open Settings → General → Analytics in the game and switch analytics off. We stop collecting gameplay analytics from that moment. The game works the same either way.

How do I delete my account and data?

Follow the steps on the Delete My Data page. Uninstalling the game does not delete your account.

Someone has an offensive display name. What do I do?

Use Report a Player and copy the name exactly as it appears. We review every report.

Where can I talk to other pilots?

Join the Cosmic Shore Discord. The dev team posts news and playtest calls there first. Discord is a community space; for account or privacy requests, please email support so we can check it’s you.
