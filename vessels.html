How a vessel works

Four elements, one kit

Collect crystals to raise an element. The higher it is, the stronger that ability. At level five each ability gets its upgrade.

TimeSpeed and the line you take: boosts, drifts and dashes.
ChargeWhat fires and goes off: guns, rockets, bombs.
MassThe prisms you lay and carry: walls, shields, trails.
SpaceRoom around you: reach, range, the cell you clear.
